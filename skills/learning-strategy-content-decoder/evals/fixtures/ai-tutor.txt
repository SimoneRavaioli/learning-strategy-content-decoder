Our pilot gives adult learners an AI tutor that proposes practice scenarios based on their stated career goals. Learners can accept, reject, or edit every recommendation. Instructors review the tutor's explanations and can intervene when a learner appears to be skipping foundational work.

Early interviews suggest that the scenarios feel more relevant than generic exercises, but we do not yet know whether the system improves retention or transfer. Several learners said the instant hints sometimes made it too easy to avoid wrestling with a difficult problem.
